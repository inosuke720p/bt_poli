Privacy Policy

Battery Expert (Android app)  •  Effective date: October 9, 2026

Summary

Battery Expert shows information about your phone's battery, tests charging, and sends battery alerts. The app does not collect, upload, sell or share your personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Everything stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Battery Expert is developed by MR. INOSUKE (“we”, “us”). Website: inosuke.online.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone number, contacts or account details.
  • No location, photos, files, microphone or camera data.
  • No advertising ID, device ID or analytics / crash-tracking data.
  • No data is sent to our servers or to any third party, because the app does not request internet permission.

3. Data used on your device

To work, the app reads battery data provided by Android (level, charging status, charger type, voltage, temperature, charging current, capacity and, where available, charge cycles) and device details such as brand, model and Android version. This is shown to you on screen and is not transmitted anywhere.

The following is stored locally on your phone only:

  • Your settings (theme, alert switches, low-battery level, background-service switch).
  • Recent charging history (sessions from about the last 3 days).
  • Latest battery values used by home-screen widgets.
  • Your choice to accept this policy, and your phone's max charging speed if you enter it.

Results of the “Test cable / adapter” feature are kept in memory while you use it and are not saved or shared.

All of this is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

4. Permissions

5. Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries. If you tap the Privacy Policy link, your browser opens this page; that visit is handled by your browser and our website host, not by the app.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Since we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your control

You can turn off notifications and the background service in the app's Settings, revoke permissions in Android settings, and delete all stored data at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above, and ask for your agreement again inside the app where required.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: visit inosuke.online.
